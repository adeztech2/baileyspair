STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

How to Link Your WhatsApp

Follow these steps to connect your WhatsApp account to ADEZ NEXUS.

01

Enter your phone number

Enter your full international phone number. For Kenya, start with 254. Do not include the plus sign.

02

Generate your pairing code

Click Get Pairing Code and wait for your unique code to appear.

03

Open WhatsApp

Android: Three-dot menu → Linked Devices.
iPhone: Settings → Linked Devices.

04

Select Link a Device

Choose Link with phone number instead if that option appears.

05

Enter your pairing code

Enter the code generated by this website into WhatsApp. Wait for the linking process to finish.

06

Copy your SESSION_ID

Once linked, copy your SESSION_ID and add it to your WhatsApp bot hosting environment as SESSION_ID.

IMPORTANT SECURITY NOTICE

Never share your pairing code or SESSION_ID with anyone. Keep your session private and secure.
