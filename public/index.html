Need to know

Common questions.

Is my WhatsApp password stored?

No. This flow uses WhatsApp’s device-linking protocol. Pairing files are temporary and removed after the session is created.

Why does the code expire?

Pairing codes are short-lived by design. If it expires, start over and generate a fresh code.

Where do I put the session ID?

Add it as the SESSION_ID environment variable on your ADEZ NEXUS bot service, then redeploy the bot.

Why does pairing need Render or a VPS?

The WhatsApp socket must remain alive while you link your phone. Static/serverless hosts such as Vercel can serve this page, but they cannot reliably keep that connection running.
